Privacy Policy

Personal bank-data integration (Actual Budget) · Effective date: 20 October 2026

1. Who operates this integration

This is a private, non-commercial integration operated by a single private individual in Norway for managing their own household finances. No service is offered to the public or to any third party. Contact: github.com/hail2victors.

2. What data is accessed

Through the bank's PSD2 interface (via the Enable Banking connector) the integration accesses account information and transaction history for the operator's own bank accounts. Access is read-only: the integration cannot initiate payments or transfers.

3. Purpose

Data is used solely for personal budgeting in the operator's own self-hosted budget application (Actual Budget). Data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

4. Where data is stored

Data is stored on hardware operated by the private individual (self-hosted). Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. No bank passwords or BankID codes are stored by the integration: authentication takes place in the bank's own login interface, and the bank issues an access token to the application. Application credentials (such as an app private key) remain under the operator's control and are not published.

5. Parties involved in data access

  • The user's bank (source of the data)
  • Enable Banking (the technical PSD2 connectivity provider)
  • The bank's identity provider (e.g., BankID), used by the user to log in

No other third parties receive data.

6. Consent, access and deletion

Bank data access relies on consent granted by the user in the bank's own interface. Consent is valid for up to 90 days and can be renewed by re-authorising the connection, or ended at any time by revoking it in the bank or by removing the connection. All collected data lives in the operator's self-hosted application and is deleted at the operator's discretion.
