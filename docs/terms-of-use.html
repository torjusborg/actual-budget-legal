Personal bank-data integration (Actual Budget) · Effective date: 20 October 2026

1. Nature of this integration

This is a private, personal integration operated by a single private individual in Norway to read their own bank data into their own self-hosted budget application (Actual Budget) under the EU PSD2 framework, using the Enable Banking connector. It is not a commercial service; no service is provided to any third party, and no availability or support is offered to anyone other than the operator. Contact: github.com/hail2victors.

2. Eligibility and scope of access

Access is limited to bank accounts owned by the operator. Using this integration with accounts belonging to other persons is prohibited.

3. Read-only access

The integration performs read operations only (account information and transactions). Payment initiation and transfers are out of scope and prohibited.

4. Credentials

The user authenticates with their bank (e.g., via BankID) in the bank's own interface. No bank passwords or BankID codes are shared with or stored by the integration. The user is responsible for safeguarding application credentials, such as the application's private key.

5. Consent

Bank data access relies on consent granted in the bank's own interface. Consent is valid for up to 90 days and can be renewed by re-authorising the connection. Revoking consent stops further data access.
